**Clock out. Put on a disc. Become the leading man.**

*Neon Afterhours* is a fast, lethal top-down action game about a middle-aged salaryman escaping into Hong Kong cinema. Every scene has a goal: fight, recover evidence, or get out alive. Taking down every enemy isn't always the answer. I made it as a hobby project in my spare time.

![Neon Afterhours cover art](/assets/images/2026-10-24-neon-afterhours/cover.png)

- **Improvise to survive.** Use doors, windows, and tables to turn the odds in your favor.
- **Build your collection.** Bring posters, figurines, soundtracks, and ability-granting outfits back to your apartment.
- **Play instantly.** The public beta runs in your browser with keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.
